# Sharing and Feedback

### Overall Evaluation

**Satisfaction level:** Satisfied.

**1. Working Environment**  
The workspace at the AWS Vietnam office (Bitexco) is quiet, tidy, and well suited for focusing on labs and team collaboration. Practicing directly on a real AWS account helped me understand how services interact and how costs behave in production.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
Mentors and the FCAJ admin team were responsive and published clear evaluation and submission guidelines. Rather than giving away ready-made fixes, mentors guided interns toward checking logs and documentation first, which strengthened independent problem-solving.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As an Artificial Intelligence (AI) major, working with AWS AI services such as Amazon Rekognition, Bedrock, and SageMaker aligned closely with my studies, while also filling practical gaps in VPC networking, IAM permissions, Serverless design, and CloudWatch monitoring.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Over 12 weeks, I strengthened my ability to read English AWS documentation, apply the AWS Well-Architected Framework, model costs with AWS Pricing Calculator, debug via CloudWatch Logs, and collaborate effectively. Building the final ImageGuard workshop taught me the most because every issue had to be diagnosed end-to-end.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Community events such as *Buildrathon Kickoff*, *AWS Cloud and AI Day Watch Party*, and *AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026* offered great networking with industry practitioners and fellow students. Within our project team, members aligned early on scope, shared notes, and cross-reviewed deliverables.

**6. Internship Policies & Benefits**  
The flexible schedule worked well alongside university coursework, while office access, offline AWS events, and the structured First Cloud AI Journey curriculum provided strong practical value.

---

### Additional Questions

* **What satisfied me most:** Designing, securing, testing, and deploying a real serverless application end-to-end on AWS alongside my team.
* **Areas for improvement in future cohorts:**
  * New AWS accounts start with a zero quota on some AI services (such as Amazon Bedrock) and quota increase requests can be denied, forcing mid-project pivots; early notice or pre-enabled lab accounts would save significant time.
  * Sharing group submission requirements (summary `.md` and architecture `.xml`) earlier helps teams prepare deliverables smoothly from the start.
  * Several AWS Console screens have changed compared to older lab guides, so refreshing screenshots would make onboarding smoother.
* **Would I recommend the program to friends?** Yes. It provides hands-on AWS practice, supportive mentorship, and valuable community exposure. I would advise newcomers to review cloud basics early and keep a steady weekly lab pace.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Suggestion:** Add a short mid-term architecture review session so teams can refine designs before heavy implementation.
* **Future participation:** I would be glad to join upcoming FCAJ community activities or workshops when my academic schedule permits.
* **Other comments:** Thank you to the FCAJ team, mentors, and admins for a rewarding and practical internship experience.
