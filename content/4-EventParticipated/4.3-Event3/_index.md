# Summary Report: “AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026”

### Event Objectives

- Host a flagship annual technology festival for students passionate about **Cloud Computing** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** in Vietnam, co-organized under the spirit *"by students, for students"* by the alliance of **AWS Student Builder Groups (SBG)** across four universities in Ho Chi Minh City (**HUFLIT**, **HCMUTE**, **PTIT**, **SGU**) alongside the **AWS First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** community.
- Equip students with practical, real-world knowledge on cloud computing, enterprise cloud security, and AI directly from industry speakers and student community leaders.
- Share authentic community-building journeys, study methods, hands-on lab experiences, and project insights across university AWS Student Builder Group chapters.
- Expand professional networking among tech-focused students, speakers, and mentors in Ho Chi Minh City through interactive Q&A sessions and career-oriented discussions in the Cloud & AI era.

### Organizers & Speakers

- **AWS / FCAJ Community Representatives & Industry Speakers**: Presented *Journeying with Young Builders* and the technical session *Enterprise Cloud Environment Security*.
- **AWS Student Builder Group HCMUTE** Representatives – Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology and Education.
- **AWS Student Builder Group SGU** Representatives – Saigon University.
- **AWS Student Builder Group HUFLIT** Representatives – Ho Chi Minh City University of Foreign Languages and Information Technology.
- **AWS Student Builder Group PTIT** Representatives – Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (HCMC Campus).

### Key Highlights

#### Check-in, Opening Ceremony & Young Builders Keynote (08:30 – 09:20)

- **08:30 – 09:00 | Guest Check-in & Welcome**: Registration, welcome kit pickup, and early networking at the AWS Vietnam office (Level 26, Bitexco Financial Tower).

- **09:10 – 09:20 | Journeying with Young Builders**: Highlighted how AWS and community initiatives such as First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) support students in progressing from classroom fundamentals to hands-on cloud building and early career readiness.

#### Technical Session: Enterprise Cloud Environment Security (09:20 – 09:40)

- **09:20 – 09:40 | Enterprise Cloud Environment Security**:
  - **Shared Responsibility Model**: Clarified the boundary between AWS's responsibility (*Security of the Cloud*) and the organization's responsibility (*Security in the Cloud*) for identities, data, networks, and applications.
  - **Identity & Access Management (IAM)**: Emphasized enforcing the principle of Least Privilege, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and structured access governance in enterprise cloud environments.
  - **Data Protection, Network Isolation & Continuous Monitoring**: Covered encryption at rest and in transit, secure VPC boundaries, and continuous auditing/threat detection practices (such as CloudTrail, GuardDuty, and CloudWatch) required in production enterprise workloads.

### Key Takeaways

#### Design Mindset

- **Security-First Mindset**: Security cannot be treated as an afterthought after deployment; it must be embedded from the initial architecture and design phase on the cloud.
- **Power of Community-Driven Learning**: Engaging with groups like AWS Student Builder Groups sustains learning momentum, fosters peer accountability, and connects students with mentors.
- **Theory to Hands-On Execution**: Academic concepts create real value when validated through hands-on labs, collaborative builds, and enterprise-grade standards.

#### Technical Architecture

- Solidified understanding of enterprise cloud security pillars: the **Shared Responsibility Model**, **IAM Least Privilege**, data encryption, network segmentation, and continuous logging/monitoring.
- Recognized how foundational security governance pairs with modern architectures (Serverless, Containers, AI/Data pipelines) to keep systems both scalable and resilient.
- Mapped a clearer learning progression from AWS cloud fundamentals to production-ready security and architecture practices.

#### Adoption Strategy

- Audit IAM permissions, resource policies, TLS/HTTPS configurations, and monitoring/logging before deploying prototypes to shared or production-like environments.
- Proactively leverage cross-university workshops, study jams, and Q&A sessions to resolve technical gaps early.

### Applying to Work

- **Strengthening Security in Internship Labs & Projects**: Apply Least Privilege when defining IAM roles and policies, protect API endpoints and storage resources, and maintain disciplined logging and resource cleanup habits.
- **Connecting Security with Modern Cloud Stacks**: Combine the enterprise security principles highlighted at the event with ongoing hands-on work in Serverless, Containers (EKS/CodePipeline), and CloudFront/ACM during the final weeks of the internship.
- **Staying Active in the Builder Community**: Continue participating in **AWS Student Builder Groups Vietnam** and **FCAJ** activities to stay current on Cloud & AI practices and grow my professional network beyond the internship.

- The **Quiz Quest** and **Word Quest** minigames kept the room lively while reinforcing Cloud and AI concepts and giving attendees a fun chance to win exclusive swag.
